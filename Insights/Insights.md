# E-Commerce Sales Analysis — Key Insights

## Database Overview
- Total Tables: 8
- Total Orders: 99,441
- Total Customers: 99,441
- Total Products: 32,951
- Total Sellers: 3,095
- Total Revenue: 16,008,872 Brazilian Real

## Key Business Insights

### Revenue
- Total platform revenue stands at 16,008,872 Brazilian Real
- Average order value is approximately 160 Brazilian Real

### Customer Insights
- 97% customers (93,099) are one-time buyers — major retention problem
- Only 3% customers (2,997) are repeat buyers
- Sao Paulo is the top city with 15,540 orders
- Top customer spent 13,664 Brazilian Real

### Product Insights
- Bed/Bath/Table (cama_mesa_banho) is the most ordered category with 11,115 orders
- 32,951 unique products available on platform

### Seller Insights
- 3,095 active sellers on the platform
- Significant variation in delivery times across sellers

### Business Recommendations
- Focus on customer retention — 97% one-time buyer rate is very high
- Sao Paulo is key market — targeted campaigns needed
- Bed/Bath/Table category needs strong inventory management
- Repeat buyer rewards program should be introduced
